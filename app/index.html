Add loot

Category and location stay put between adds, so a whole loot run goes in fast. Adding the same item to the same location stacks the quantity. Materials accept decimals (SCU).

Inventory

Ship component sizes and grades come from the item database. Press Update to load them.

Blueprints

Loot by location

Built from the places you log with “Looted in” and “Place or site” when adding items. Pick a system, or type a place, to see what you found there.

Finder

Shops and prices come from UEX. Blueprint missions come from the Star Citizen Wiki. Both are community-maintained, so check the dates shown.

Missing-blueprint planner

Ranks missions by how many blueprints you have not marked as known. The mission data comes from the Star Citizen Wiki. Each reward pool is treated as “one blueprint from this list”, so the per-run figure is an estimate. Mark blueprints as known on the Blueprints tab (or here) to keep it accurate.

Craft list

Press “+ Craft list” on any blueprint in the Blueprints tab. This adds up the materials for everything on the list and compares them with what you own.

History

Item database

Update downloads the full item and blueprint lists from the Star Citizen Wiki API and the UEX API straight into your browser, then adds anything new to autocomplete. The first update takes about a minute and downloads roughly 60 MB; the database is then kept in your browser. Neither source is run by this tool, and either can be briefly unavailable. Nothing you track is ever sent anywhere.

Backup & share

Saved automatically to a file on this computer, with a dated backup copy made once a day (the last 14 are kept). Reinstalling or updating the app does not touch it.

Your inventory and known blueprints are saved in this browser only. They do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data erases them. Copy the backup text and keep it somewhere safe, or paste it on another device to move your list.
