Item database

Update downloads the full item and blueprint lists from the Star Citizen Wiki API and the UEX API straight into your browser, then adds anything new to autocomplete. The first update takes about a minute and downloads roughly 60 MB; the database is then kept in your browser. Neither source is run by this tool, and either can be briefly unavailable. Nothing you track is ever sent anywhere.

Backup & share

Saved automatically to a file on this computer, with a dated backup copy made once a day (the last 14 are kept). Reinstalling or updating the app does not touch it.

Your inventory and known blueprints are saved in this browser only. They do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data erases them. Copy the backup text and keep it somewhere safe, or paste it on another device to move your list.
